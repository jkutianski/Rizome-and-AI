# AI Seen Through a Rhizome

**AI Seen Through a Rhizome** is a project of reflection and exploration on the expansion of artificial intelligence and the transformations it produces in the relationships between science, technology, and society.

The project revisits the perspective originally developed in [**Technology Seen Through a Rhizome**](https://latintaenlasopa.blogspot.com/2010/10/la-tecnologia-vista-desde-un-rizoma.html), written in 2010, and explores it again in the context of contemporary artificial intelligence.

## A Rhizomatic Perspective

The notion of the **rhizome**, inspired by Gilles Deleuze and Félix Guattari, is not used here to claim that artificial intelligence, technology, or society *are* a rhizome.

It is used as a **way of observing**.

A way of paying attention to the relationships, connections, tensions, dependencies, and transformations that emerge between elements that, when viewed in isolation, may appear to be independent.

From this perspective, the project develops its own **cartographic method** for exploring these relationships.

The method does not attempt to construct a rhizome or produce a *rhizomatic cartography*. Instead, it constructs a **partial map** that makes certain relationships visible within a dynamic field.

And the map is not the territory.

The map is a construction made by the observer: it selects, connects, simplifies, and organizes. The territory, meanwhile, continues to transform, incorporating new elements and producing relationships that may not be represented on the map.

For this reason, the result is not intended to be a definitive representation of artificial intelligence, but rather a **provisional cartography of some of the relationships that emerge around it**.

## The Project

The project explores different types of relationships and tensions that can emerge around artificial intelligence, including:

* science and technology
* technology and society
* knowledge and automation
* humans and AI systems
* production and transformation
* dependence and autonomy
* concentration and distribution
* creation and reorganization
* opportunities and tensions

These relationships are not considered closed categories. The map can change as new connections, perspectives, or contradictions emerge.

The goal is not to close the map, but to **make visible a field that remains open**.

## From Text to Map

The project starts from an essay written in 2010 about technology and revisits it from the perspective of the present state of artificial intelligence.

The visualization makes it possible to explore the proposed relationships in a way that differs from linear text: not as a single sequence of arguments, but as a space where different elements can connect, separate, and encounter one another again.

The visualization should be understood as an **exploratory tool**, not as an exhaustive representation of the phenomenon.

## An Open Investigation

**AI Seen Through a Rhizome** does not seek to offer a definitive theory of artificial intelligence.

Nor does it seek to determine in advance which relationships are the most important.

The project proposes a perspective and builds tools for exploring it.

The map can change.

The relationships can change.

The territory can change as well.
